Audit of the old WordPress site (WP Engine, pre-rebuild). Site score 4/10, SEO 3/10.

**What was good (keep in rebuild):**
- Recovery amounts front and center: $728M AL Opioid, $370M BCBS, $2.67B Consumer Fraud, $5M Defective Products, $3M Medical Injury, $3M Abuse
- 30+ practice area sub-pages — extensive content architecture
- Richard's founder story ("We don't all have a best friend that is a lawyer — but now, you do")
- 3-step process (Fill Out Form → Free Consultation → Speak with Lawyer)
- Active blog / Legal News section

**Critical issues the rebuild fixes:**
1. Four live copy errors: "millions recoverd" (typo), "an you guarantee" (missing C), "law firm the works with" (missing "that"), "sTEP" inconsistent caps in 3-step process
2. Duplicate recovery ticker amounts ($5M, $3M, $1M each showing twice — WP slider bug)
3. No schema markup at all on the WP site (LegalService, Organization, FAQ — none)
4. Experience years inconsistent: "25+ Years" in Why Us vs "over thirty five years" in bio — use 35+ everywhere
5. Richard's AV Preeminent 5.0, Super Lawyers (9x), PIABA Foundation Board, 2 published books NOT surfaced on homepage

**Why:** The Svelte rebuild addresses all of these. Schema, speed, copy accuracy, and credentials are now handled.

**How to apply:** When adding or editing content, ensure 35+ years is used consistently. Credentials (AV Preeminent, Super Lawyers 9x, PIABA Board, 2 books) should appear in founder sections. Recovery amounts should all be present and not duplicated.
